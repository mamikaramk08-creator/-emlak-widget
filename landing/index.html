Questions

Before you switch, the honest answers.

Will this sound robotic to my clients?

No — the AI writes in plain language, not canned scripts. And on the Inbox Triage plan, every reply is drafted for your review before it's sent, so nothing goes out without your voice on it.

Is my client data secure?

Yes. Your data stays in your own account and is never shared with or sold to third parties.

Do I need a developer to set this up?

No. The widget is a single line added to your site, and inbox triage is a direct inbox connection — both are live in under 5 minutes.

Will this replace me or my team?

No. It doesn't negotiate or close deals — it does the qualifying and sorting, so you show up to a prioritized, ready-to-work lead list instead of a buried inbox.

What if the AI gets something wrong?

On Inbox Triage, every reply is a draft — you approve it before it sends. On the widget, you can take over any conversation directly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, anytime after the 3-month minimum, no long-term lock-in.
